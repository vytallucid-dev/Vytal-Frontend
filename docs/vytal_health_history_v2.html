How to read this
Health — the quality of the businesses in your book we can score, from 0 to 100. It answers "are the things I own sound?" It only reflects the part of your book that's individual companies.
Construction — how well your whole book is built: concentration, breadth, balance. From 0 to 100. It answers "is this book safely put together?" — independent of the businesses' quality.
They move independently and are never combined. A book can hold great businesses assembled riskily (high Health, low Construction) — or the reverse. Watching them apart is the point.
